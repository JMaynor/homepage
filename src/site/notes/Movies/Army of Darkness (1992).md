# Review

Smile on face! Smile on face!

Sam Raimi really said "I will move heaven and Earth to construct a scenario where a buncha skeletons are sieging a castle." And he was right for that. I was actually a little bit cold on Army of Darkness for the first like 40 or so minutes. I'm speaking on a curve, of course. So I was openly smiling for much of it, just not laughing. [[Movies/Evil Dead II (1987)\|Evil Dead 2]] had me by the *throat*. Constantly cackling for the entirety of its runtime. Army of Darkness was more subdued in its laughs. It was more continuously absurd. Rather than having peaks of hilarity. But I think once we really get into it, especially once we're in the main event. That aforementioned surge. We find our stride and all becomes clear. Ash is beloved. I enjoy that he went from just a guy to a total insane maniac over the space of about 2-3 days.
