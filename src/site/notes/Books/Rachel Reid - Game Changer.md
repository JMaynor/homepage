# Reading Notes

Haven't been fucking with it thus far. We've got a big case of "everyone's perfect and we don't go anywhere/do anything". My bane in this damn genre. Shame, will have to see if rest of book or next book is better. Surely must be if it got a TV adaptation...

# Review

Got better after a while, though I feel my initial reservations were not wrong. The book was not bad overall. I just want things to be more. I want there to be stakes and movements and depth. I want Mary Renault to get a little dirty.
