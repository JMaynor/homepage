# Review

They're just not really great, this series. Too easy a read, I always find myself wanting more. Wanting more depth, more characterization. Every time these people end up together I can but think "You know literally nothing about each other..."

I understand why I may have quit here. I can very much see it. I think they were the first ones I read over that other series. Surprised I tried anything else.
