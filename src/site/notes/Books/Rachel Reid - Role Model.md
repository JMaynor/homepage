# Review

Not bad. Was firmly in land of becomes less interesting the moment the couple is fully together.

Kinda funny how interconnected everything's gotten in this series. It is a conspicuously small world. I do enjoy Ilya as this Nick Fury approaching our various little gay superheroes and demanding they get their shit together. He's a bit *too* perfect but I kinda like him so I'll let it slide.
