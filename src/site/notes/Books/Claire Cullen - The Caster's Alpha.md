# Review

Is that bickering? Enemies to lovers? I mean, not really but it's a direction we gesture in which I guess is better than usual. I also like that we physically go places. That's nice. Events happen. I still don't really like, but I can be giving and say that it shows improvement. (Obviously, we end up at the same place we always do. I love you and always have, take me now!)

I think the most frustrating thing about this series is that I absolutely could not differentiate one of the alphas from another. Or one of the mages from another. They are all exactly the same. None have a voice or an actual personality. None are people. It just sucks. Whatever, done with it now.
