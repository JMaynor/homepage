# Review

I think I wanna move on from this series. It's got good stuff. Definitely on the higher end of these sorts of books that I've read. But I think I've read enough of them in succession that I've tired of it. They're fairly formulaic and I gotta get out there a bit more with my reading. Doesn't necessarily need to be the high diction of the classics, but a little more variety is needed.

Anyway, book itself is fine. It just feels largely unnecessary to me. We struggle to get a full plot going at the best of times with these sorts of books. I never wanted a sequel to any of them. Just seemed like a bad idea. Too much treading water.
