# Review

In comparison to some of the other erotica I've read, I like that the characters feel a bit deeper. There's a bit more to each of them than you sometimes get. Though I do feel we run into the same problem here that I do in books by [[People/K.M. Neuhold\|K.M. Neuhold]] or [[People/Eliot Grayson\|Eliot Grayson]] (examples of other similar authors whose stuff I've read). I'm wanting something more substantial than I'm getting. I want a bit more plot, where the you know what just is another part of the total experience.
