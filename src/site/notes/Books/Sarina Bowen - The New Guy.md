# Review

I enjoyed. I think I like this lady's stuff. We get deeper into the characters. There's like actual drama. It's not *that* deep. But it at least gets you going. More than some of the nothings I've read in other romance books. People have actual personalities. We're not 3d. Not even close to that. But people will have thoughts and do actions that don't totally align with like 2-3 vague personality traits. As though they perhaps have thoughts going on in their heads.
