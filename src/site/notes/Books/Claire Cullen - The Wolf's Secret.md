# Review

I think this one is slightly better than the others because we've at least got the smallest modicum of internal debate going on. There is some miniscule character development to be had. We're not like orders of magnitude better. But I feel some sense of improvement. I do have to say that the larger plot stuff at the end with the "archmage" and the balance of magic in the world made me smile from how silly it was. We all know that the next book is going to consist of the same thing as the last several books.
