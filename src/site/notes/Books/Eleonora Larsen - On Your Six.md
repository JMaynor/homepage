# Review

Close enough, welcome back GhostSoap. I kid! Honestly, I quite liked this one. I'll say, I respected the structure of the books. The time skips between the parts. Made it feel much more grounded than you usually get in a romance. Would definitely put On Your Six in the top list of romances I've read. I do still desire something more big novel length, that search hasn't ended. But this was nice. Felt like we were occasionally willing to have stakes. Nothing exceptional, but it's not a rosy perfect oopy goopy everything is perfect story. And that makes me happy. I *need* romances that are at least willing to go *somewhere*. In this one we do go places and we do do things. Which is not something that can be said for a good number of romances I've read. Great first book for an author, definitely interested in reading more.
