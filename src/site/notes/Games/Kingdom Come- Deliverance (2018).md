# Summary

Kingdom Come: Deliverance (that feels like it should be illegal. Can you put a colon in the title of the first game in a series? Feels like putting the cart before the horse to me...) is an open world RPG set during the funny Pentiment times. Where, like, we are technically becoming a society and advanced and stuff but it really doesn't feel like it. The game was developed and published by Warhorse Studios as well as Deep Silver and was released in 2018. In it, you play as a blacksmith's son who is thrust into a civil war. Dreadfully common these days innit?

Don't know too much about this one. Sam requested it. Saw him playing a bit of it on stream once and it recalled Oblivion, which I do like.
